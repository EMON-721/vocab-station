Vocab Station practice examples

Original teaching examples, adaptations, and sense-specific corrections:
Vocab Station, 2026.

Additional English definitions and example sentences adapted from:
Oxford3000_Vocab by Mustafa D. (2024)
https://github.com/ciwga/Oxford3000_Vocab
Dataset: oxford3000_vocabulary_with_collocations_and_definitions_datasets.csv
The repository describes its dataset as generated with GPT-4 assistance.
It is an independent learning resource, not an official Oxford dictionary publication.
Existing Vocab Station examples and learner/admin edits take priority.
